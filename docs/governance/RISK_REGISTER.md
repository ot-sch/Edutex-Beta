## Risk acceptance rule

Scores 15-25 require treatment before live use and executive/security acceptance of any remainder.
Scores 8-12 require a treatment owner/date and at least security-owner acceptance. Scores 1-6 may be
accepted by the service owner if monitored. No one may accept a risk that breaches law, contract or
an absolute organisational policy.

Review on every release, monthly during production-candidate testing, after any incident/material
supplier change, and at least quarterly in steady state.

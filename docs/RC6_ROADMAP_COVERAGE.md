# RC6 roadmap coverage and release blockers

5 September 2026 | Compared with the supplied **Edutex Roadmap Requirements** document and RC5
source.

**The full roadmap is not complete. This is an implemented review build, not a production acceptance
or certification statement.** No existing feature was intentionally removed. The deliberate access
restrictions on community accounts, internal forms, fee visibility and immutable finance/consent
history correct privacy or integrity defects that conflict with the roadmap.

“Implemented” below means the named behaviour exists in the delivered source. Local tests and
synthetic browser review cover the cases listed in `quality/RELEASE_VERIFICATION.md`; they do not
certify every role, device, cloud service or business process. “Partial” identifies useful code with
specific missing behaviour. No percentage-complete claim is made.

ISO's catalogue identifies
[19788-1:2024 as the replacement for the withdrawn 2011 framework](https://www.iso.org/standard/50772.html).
Its [29187-1:2013 page](https://www.iso.org/standard/45266.html) describes a published standard
confirmed in 2025 with revision work underway. Obtain the relevant standards and agree the exact
scope/editions before undertaking conformance assessment; this project has not reviewed the full
licensed texts.

## What must happen before a complete-roadmap claim

1. Design and implement the all-data client-encryption architecture, safe existing-data conversion
   and corresponding search, reporting, background processing, key recovery and revocation model. Do
   not silently relax this requirement.
2. Agree an achievable endpoint/device threat model for event medical access; the absolute
   no-capture condition cannot be met by a general browser or by preventing a camera from viewing a
   screen.
3. Finish the missing finance, native/offline, query/automation, Classroom, alumni and specialised
   reporting workflows listed above; define and test their external integration contracts.
4. Complete school-specific privacy/clinical/accounting acceptance and the actual cloud, identity,
   delivery, backup/restore, device/accessibility, load and independent security tests.
5. Undertake the organisation's applicable standards assessment and certification process. Source
   code and a generated checklist cannot substitute for operating evidence.
